Abstract
We study immersive video-to-audio (V2A) by generating
first-order ambisonics (FOA) from silent field-of-view (FoV)
videos. Challenges include sparse semantics in video-FOA
corpora and content-geometry entanglement: end-to-end mod-
els blur ”what” and ”where”, while two-stage pipelines trade
semantic fidelity for spatial coherence. We present Foley-
Immersive and a semantics-augmented dataset, YT-AmbiSem,
which augments YT-Ambigen with structured descriptions from
Qwen2.5-VL-7B. Stage 1 (WHAT) uses a semantics-first diffu-
sion model with multi-rate cross-frame attention and probabilis-
tic time modulation to generate mono W. Stage 2 (WHERE)
spatializes W to XYZ via a complex-STFT U-Net conditioned
on per-frame visuals and camera direction; a lightweight direc-
tional residual mixer applies view-dependent bottleneck gating
to stabilize localization with minimal content drift. We achieve
state-of-the-art semantic and spatial metrics. Demo and dataset
are available.1
Index Terms: Spatial audio, ambisonics, video-to-audio gener-
ation, multimodal learning, dataset
1. Introduction
Spatial audio is central to immersion in video, XR, and inter-
active media [1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7]. Beyond producing plausible
sounds, animmersivesystem must also place sounds at percep-
tually consistent directions in 3D [8, 9, 10]. A pragmatic path
is togeneratefirst-order ambisonics (FOA) from silent videos,
enabling automatic Foley for in-the-wild content without Am-
bisonic capture hardware [11, 12, 13, 14].
Two lines of progress.Prior work on vision-conditioned
audio spatializationassumes areference audiois available and
uses video to transform mono to binaural/ambisonics. Early
milestones include self-supervised 360 ◦ video spatialization
that separates and localizes sources on the viewing sphere [11],
Mono2Binauralfor NFOV videos using FAIR-Play data [15],
and unified separation and stereo generation (Sep-Stereo) that
leverages abundant mono data with a shared A V backbone [16].
Subsequent work further removed the need for real binaural
labels via pseudo-binaural supervision [12]. While effective,
these methods require an input track and thus do not solve gen-
eration from silence.
A second line,immersive V2A, aims todirectlygenerate
spatial audio from silent video. Diff-SAGe introduced a condi-
tional diffusion-transformer that synthesizes FOA from high-
level conditions (class, source location, camera) [17]. ViS-
AGe framed video-to-FOA from ordinary FoV videos and re-

end-to-end model leverages CLIP semantics and an autoregres-

What remains difficult.Despite rapid progress, two gaps
persist when extending V2A from mono to FOA: (i)Data side:
public video–FOA corpora carry limited textual semantics and
sparse temporal cues, weakening grounding forwhat to sound.
(ii)System side:end-to-end spatial generators may entangle
content and geometry, while naive two-stage pipelines often
trade off: stronger spatialization can distort semantics; weaker
spatialization loses directionality.
Our premise.We decouplewhatandwhere: Stage 1 fo-
cuses onsemantics-firstmono generation (W), and Stage 2 per-
formsminimal-interferencespatialization (W→XYZ). This
preserves content while improving spatial coherence.
We present FOLEYIMMERSIVE, a modular two-stage
framework and a semantics-augmented dataset YT-AMBISEM
to advance immersive V2A on FoV videos.
In summary, our contributions are as follows:
•YT-AMBISEM:A semantics-augmented FOA dataset with
structured textual descriptions (events, objects, environment)
to enhance audio-visual grounding.
•Decoupled Two-Stage Architecture:
–Stage 1 (What):A semantics-first diffusion model with
Multi-Rate Cross-Frame Attention (MR-CFA) and Prob-
abilistic Time Modulation (PTM) for mono generation.
–Stage 2 (Where):A complex-STFT spatializer with a Di-
rectional Residual Mixer (DRM) for minimal-interference
spatialization.
•SOTA Results:Our method achieves state-of-the-art perfor-
mance on both semantic and spatial metrics in the Video-to-
First-Order Ambisonics task.

2.2. Overall Architecture
Our system FOLEYIMMERSIVEdecouples content and geome-
try in two stages (Fig. 1). Stage 1 (WHAT) generates the omni
channelWconditioned on multi-rate visual features and struc-
tured text, and is then frozen to produce ˆW. Stage 2 (WHERE)
predictsX/Y/Zin the complex-STFT domain from ˆW, in-
jecting only lightweight vision- and direction-gated residuals at
the bottleneck to avoid rewriting the semantics carried by ˆW.
We train Stage 2 with ground-truthWand occasionally substi-
tute/perturb ˆWin later epochs to mitigate train–test mismatch.

3. Experiments and Results
3.1. Datasets, Metrics
Datasets.Our model is trained on YT-AMBISEM(the
semantics-augmented version of YT-Ambigen).
Metrics.Following the evaluation method in [18], we report
two families of metrics. (1)Semantic quality: FOA→mono
decoding along(φ,θ), then computeFAD dec (Fr´echet Audio
Distance) andKLD dec (mean KL divergence); we also re-
port the average FAD as FAD avg [30]. (2)Spatiality: correla-
tion (CC) and AUC between predicted vs. ground-truth audio-
energy maps at three temporal granularities (All/1 fps/5 fps)
[18]. Lower is better forFADdec, FADavg, andKLD dec; higher
is better for CC/AUC.
Implementation details.Audio is resampled to16kHz and
represented with STFT (n fft = 1024, hop= 256, Hann win-
dow). Both stages are trained with AdamW (lr= 10 −4, batch
= 32per GPU) for200epochs; Stage 1 uses50DDIM steps at
inference, while Stage 2 is single-pass.

3.4. Subjective Evaluation (MOS)
We conducted a 5-point MOS test with 30 screened graduate
listeners (headphones, quiet room) on 50 five-second videos;
for each video, FOA outputs from four systems were rendered
with the same playback setup, presented in randomized order,
and rated onSemantics(sound–content match),Spatiality(di-
rectional correctness/stability), andOverallquality. FOLEY-
IMMERSIVE achieves the best MOS (Tab. 4), surpassing ViS-
AGe by +0.31/+0.58/+0.40 and Ambi-Enc. baselines by≥1.0,
consistent with the CC/AUC gains.

4. Conclusion
We proposeFoleyImmersive, a novel video-to-first-order-
ambisonics generation framework that enhances both semantic
fidelity and spatial coherence. Our contributions include: (1)
YT-AmbiSem, a large-scale semantics-augmented dataset with
detailed multimodal annotations, (2)A decoupled two-stage
architecturewith multi-rate cross-frame attention and prob-
abilistic time modulation for precise audio-visual alignment,
and (3)Directional residual mixingfor minimal-interference
spatialization. Experiments demonstrate that FoleyImmersive
surpasses state-of-the-art methods across all evaluated seman-
tic and spatial metrics. Ablation studies confirm the effective-
ness of our multi-rate conditioning and directional mixing tech-
niques. Our approach advances immersive audio generation,
and future work will focus on improving robustness and gener-
alization to unseen and more complex acoustic scenes.

5. Use of Generative AI Disclosure
Generative AI tools were used for two purposes in this work.
First, large language models were used for language editing
during manuscript preparation, including grammar correction,
spelling and punctuation checking, and minor stylistic refine-
ment to improve clarity and fluency. Second, the multimodal
model Qwen2.5-VL-7B was used to automatically generate
structured semantic descriptions for dataset construction (YT-
AMBISEM). These descriptions were programmatically gener-
ated and used as semantic annotations in the proposed frame-
work. No AI tools were used in the design of the core method-
ology, experimental setup, result analysis, or scientific conclu-
sions. All research contributions and interpretations were devel-
oped independently by the authors, who take full responsibility
for the integrity and accuracy of this work.
