TITLE: A light weight Continuous Speaker Verification System for Real time Monitoring
PRESENTER: Harish Rajamani
ABSTRACT:
Continuous verification of customer identity during telephone interactions is essential to prevent unauthorized access to personal information in medical insurance applications and banking scenarios. We propose a real-time speaker verification system that continuously monitors customer's speech and compares it with an enrolled voice-print to detect speaker changes during an active call. Upon mismatch, the system generates alerts on the agent interface to prevent disclosure of sensitive information. The proposed method uses a two stage architecture with a ReDimNet-B1 backbone and a projection network trained with triplet sampling for robust speaker embedding extraction. Cross-lingual, noise, and reverberation-based data augmentation techniques are used to improve robustness. The system operates in real time with RTF 0.05 and a low computational footprint of 318M MACs. The model achieves an Equal Error Rate (EER) of 2.08% on TidyVoice evaluation dataset, demonstrating strong robustness.

ID: 3588
TITLE: WaveNorm: A Low-Complexity Time-Domain Neural Adaptive Gain Control for Real-Time Speech Applications
PRESENTER: Harish Rajamani
ABSTRACT:
We present WaveNorm AGC, a lightweight, low-latency neural Adaptive Gain Control(AGC) system for real-time speech normalization on edge devices. Operating in the time domain, it learns a content-aware, level-invariant gain mapping directly from raw waveforms. Unlike conventional rule-based AGC, it avoids fixed control parameters and hand-crafted envelope tracking, providing stable and responsive gain adjustment. WaveNorm AGC provides robust performance across a wide dynamic range upto -70 dB maintaining loudness compliance with ITU-T P.56 and P.79 standards under noisy conditions, while achieving ~6 dB noise reduction. The model is speaker-independent and highly efficient (49 M MACs, 55 KB of memory), making it suitable for real-time deployment on resource-constrained devices.

ID: 3602
TITLE: Argmax Pro: Frontier-level Real-time Speech-to-text with Speakers and Custom Vocabulary on Mobile Devices
PRESENTER: Atila Orhon
ABSTRACT:
We introduce Argmax Pro, a unified real-time inference system that orchestrates three state-of-the-art billion-scale transformer models for speech-to-text with speaker diarization and custom vocabulary. The system is optimized to run efficiently on a wide range of mobile devices across iOS and Android without resource contention with other apps and with minimal impact to battery life and thermals. The goal of this system is to offer a peer alternative to cloud-based systems for feature-rich speech recognition with frontier-level accuracy. To achieve this, we built Argmax Pro on top of concurrent research in context biasing and streaming speaker diarization.

TITLE: A Human-in-the-Loop Multi-Agent Companion for Real-Time Entity Extraction and SLU-Driven ASR Error Correction
PRESENTER: Shiva Shankar Arumugam
ABSTRACT:
Contact-center agents handle thousands of entity-rich voice interactions per day, where a misrecognised customer name, account identifier, or domain term cascades into wrong CRM entries and additional manual rework. We demonstrate MACE (Multi-Agent Companion Environment), a closed-loop architecture in which a real-time companion - an AI overlay that runs alongside the live agent and surfaces extracted named entities as proactive suggestions for in-place confirmation - routes every UI correction back into the ASR stack as either a contextual-biasing entry B or, once recurrent, a post-ASR substitution rule R, with no acoustic retraining and no a-priori entity list. On the English ContextASR-Bench benchmark with Whisper-large-v3, the loop reduces NE-WER by 17.1% and EditRate by 18.6% over a no-biasing baseline, closing 23.6% of the gap to an oracle that is given the entity list in advance.
